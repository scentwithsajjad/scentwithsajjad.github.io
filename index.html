NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What decant sizes are available?

5ML, 8ML and 10ML are the standard sizes shown on this website. Availability can vary by fragrance.

Does “Reviewed” mean it is available for decant?

No. Review status and decant availability are separate. A fragrance can be reviewed but not currently available as a decant.

Are these full bottles?

No. Decants are smaller portions of fragrance transferred into travel-sized atomizers. Product listings will clearly indicate the available sizes.

How do I order?

Use the WhatsApp order button on a product card. Share the fragrance name and preferred size, and confirm current availability and pricing.
